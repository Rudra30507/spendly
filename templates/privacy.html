Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects the following categories of data to provide and improve the Service:

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.

Expense Data: Any expenses you enter — including amount, category, date, description, and any attached notes — are stored in your account.

Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited, features used, time spent, and device/browser information (IP address, user agent, operating system).

Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use essential cookies to maintain your session and preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To authenticate you and secure your account
  • To send you service-related communications (e.g., account updates, security notices)
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, nor do we use your expense data for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard protections, including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Access to your data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

Expense data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses or your entire account at any time, which will permanently remove your data from our systems within 30 days.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may integrate with or rely on the following third-party services:

  • Hosting/Infrastructure: Cloud hosting providers (e.g., AWS, Vercel, or similar) to run the application
  • Authentication: If you sign in via OAuth (Google, GitHub, etc.), that provider receives your authentication request per their own privacy policy
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g., Plausible, Umami) that do not collect personally identifiable information

These third parties only process data on our behalf and are contractually obligated to protect it. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days as required by applicable law.

6. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and provide a prominent notice on the Service.

Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
